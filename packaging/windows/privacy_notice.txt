================================================================================
SYNAPR: LOCAL AIR-GAP & ZERO DATA LEAK GUARANTEE
================================================================================

Welcome to Synapr -- the Autonomous Local Multi-IDE AI Development Orchestrator.

1. 100% Local Machine Execution
   Synapr is architected from the core to operate entirely on your local
   machine. Subtask decomposition, peer debate consensus, Git worktrees,
   host IDE dispatch, automated tests, and branch reconciliation run
   locally on your system.

2. Zero Data Leak & Zero Telemetry Collection
   - Synapr DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit your source code.
   - Synapr DOES NOT collect user analytics, tracking cookies, or telemetry.
   - The web dashboard is 100% local: no external fonts, scripts, or CDNs.
   - Outbound network traffic is strictly isolated to the LLM endpoints
     that you explicitly configure in the Gateway settings.

3. Complete Ownership & Security
   Your prompts, credentials, API keys, and repository files remain in
   your complete ownership under your strict local control at all times.
